##### Date: 01/06/2026
##### Hours Completed: 4.5 hours

At Family Care Network, I was set up with a name badge and given my login credentials to start their on-boarding process.

I completed their training videos about workplace dynamics, situations dealing with chemicals, and proper medical practice when in an environment with blood. Standard procedure. 

When I was done with the training, I shadowed one of the System Analysts (Alex) who worked there. He showed the different applications used like the ticketing system, a remote desktop application called Ninja that is hosted on Citrix, the FCN wiki, and how to answer help-desk calls. In total, I saw 3 different calls; one unlocking an account for a user, another troubleshooting a monitor issue, and one troubleshooting an EKG machine. 

I had a tour of the building and met with the directors and doctors of FCN, learning that the company is owned by physicians and rotates leadership, which is very cool!
